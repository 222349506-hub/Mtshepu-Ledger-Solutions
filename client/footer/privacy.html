Privacy Policy

Mtshepu Ledger Solutions (Pty) Ltd is committed to safeguarding the privacy of our website visitors and clients. This policy outlines how we handle, process, and protect your personal and business financial data in strict compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal and financial information necessary to deliver high-quality bookkeeping services. This includes:

  • Contact Details: Names, email addresses, phone numbers, and physical business addresses.
  • Financial Documentation: Bank account statements, tax identifiers, invoices, receipts, and payroll data.
  • Technical Data: Information submitted voluntarily via our website contact forms.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is processed solely for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Fulfilling our contractual bookkeeping obligations to you.
  • Communicating with you regarding updates, ledger queries, or financial reports.
  • Processing invoices and service payments.
  • Ensuring compliance with local financial and tax reporting standards.

3. Data Security and Storage

We implement rigorous organizational, physical, and technological security measures to protect your sensitive financial records from unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. Your files are stored securely using cloud-based ledger software and encrypted databases. Access is strictly limited to authorized personnel handling your account.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial information to third parties. We will only share data with external regulatory bodies (such as SARS) when explicitly authorized by you or required to do so by South African law.

5. Your POPIA Rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to look at what information we hold about you, request corrections to inaccurate records, or ask us to delete your personal data when it is no longer required for legal or contractual purposes.

6. Contact Our Information Officer

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please email us directly at Jaymtshepu@gmail.com.
